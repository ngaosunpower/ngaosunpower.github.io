FAQ

Questions we get asked first.

Three things: electric motorbikes for riders, electric vehicles for drivers and fleet operators, and rooftop solar systems for businesses and institutions. All three are offered on a fixed instalment plan.

It depends on the asset and on your own situation, so we quote each case individually rather than publishing a single figure that would not fit everyone.

Send us an enquiry with the asset you are interested in and roughly what you earn, and we will come back with a written plan showing the deposit, the term and the payment amount. The more we know about how you will use the asset, the more accurate that plan will be.

For an individual: your Kenyan National ID, a valid KRA PIN certificate, a valid driving licence (Class A2 for a motorbike, Class B for a vehicle), and three months of M-Pesa or bank statements. A good conduct certificate is also requested.

For a business or institution we assess the entity instead — talk to us and we will confirm the exact list.

The plan covers the complete unit. When it is fully paid, title to the frame and both batteries transfers to you. This is deliberately different from rental models where you end up with a vehicle but no ownership of its batteries.

Once we have a complete set of documents, we come back with a written offer in no more than 2 days. The most common cause of delay is an incomplete application, so send everything at once and we will tell you immediately if anything is missing.

We arrange NTSA registration, the logbook and the vehicle inspection as part of the package, so the asset is legally on the road from the day you collect it. Third-party insurance and personal accident cover for the rider are discussed with you when we put the plan together.

We carry out a site and consumption assessment, design the system, then finance, install and commission it. You pay monthly over the term of the agreement, and the system is titled to you once the plan is paid up. We remain responsible for maintenance during the term.
